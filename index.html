ABOUT THIS PLACE

Not just radio.

Listening Beyond the Noise is a home for projects that cross over between radio, software, old hardware, making things, brewing things and growing things.

It is less a polished portfolio and more a working bench: experiments, build notes, photos, mistakes, fixes and finished projects — when they actually reach that stage.
